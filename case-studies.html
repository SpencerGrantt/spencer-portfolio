Case Studies

Design begins with Data.

Research comes first, always. Not just doing it, but understanding what the gathered data means. Every design decision ties to real, sourced data. My case studies listed below all show examples of data-driven design, the processes may differ but the foundation under them doesn't.

Diagnosing Alert Fatigue · Deepest case study

Designed a clinical alert dashboard to cut hospitalist override behavior

Understanding why hospitalists override so many critical alerts is not something many designers think about, but for the designers that do, purposeful design in an alert dashboard saves lives. Hospitalists are frustrated with critical alerts that look the same regardless of severity. Two rounds of usability testing, synthesized from peer-reviewed clinical sources, helped me build a dashboard that active medical professionals could act on effectively.
