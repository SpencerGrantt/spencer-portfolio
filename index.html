Designing the interface for the moment someone has to trust — or override — an AI.

Hospitalists override 95% of AI-generated drug-interaction alerts, not from carelessness, but because every alert looks the same regardless of severity. I redesign the alert, not the algorithm: severity hierarchy, AI confidence, and recommended action, readable in one glance. (Bryant et al., 2014)

Designed a clinical alert dashboard to cut hospitalist override behavior on critical drug interactions

Round 1 testing (n=3 RN proxy testers): 100% correctly triaged the critical alert, but 0% chose the correct action once AI confidence was shown. Round 2 targets that exact gap, and the first Round 2 respondent got it right.
